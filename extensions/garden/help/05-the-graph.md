# The graph

The **Graph** screen draws the whole garden as it really is: each dot a note, each line a link. Notes that link a lot grow larger, and the layout settles itself, pulling linked notes together and pushing the rest apart.

## Moving around

| Do this | And |
| --- | --- |
| Scroll the wheel | Zoom in and out |
| Drag the background | Pan |
| Drag a note | Move it; its neighbours follow |
| Hover a note | Light it and its neighbours, dim everything else |
| Click a note | Open it |
| Right-click a note | Show only its neighbourhood |
| `Ctrl` `0` | Fit the whole garden in view |

## What it can show

The row above the graph has:

- **Find**, to pick out the notes that hold what you type, in their names or their text.
- **Colour** by stage (Seed, Growing, Evergreen), by kind, or by branch: the section of the garden a note grows in, so each part of the tree has a colour of its own. A garden with a tree opens by branch, and the legend in the corner says what each colour means.
- **Tag dots**, to add every tag as a small grey dot linked to its notes. Clicking a tag dot highlights its notes; right-click it to open the tag.
- **Orphans**, to show or hide the notes with no links.
- **Arrows**, to show which way each link points.
- **Spread**, Tight, Normal or Loose, to pack the notes closer or give them room.

Links you or an agent made by hand are drawn dashed; links to tags are dotted.

## Highlight by tag

Beside the graph is every tag, with how many notes carry it, the most carried first. Click a tag and the notes that carry it keep their colour and their names, the links among them stay, and the rest fade; the view does not move. A note with several tags is highlighted whenever one of the tags you chose is among them.

- **Two tags or more** highlight the notes that carry **all of them**, so each click narrows. Choose **Any of them** to highlight the notes that carry either.
- While you narrow, the number beside each other tag says how many of the highlighted notes carry it, and a tag that would leave nothing is greyed.
- **A colour in the legend** highlights the notes of that branch, stage or kind, and together with tags, the notes that are both. **Find** narrows the highlight too.
- Hover a note to see its own neighbours over the highlight. **Esc** or **Clear the highlight** ends it. The × hides the tags; **Tags** brings them back.

> The most linked notes are always named; the other labels fade in as you zoom. Zoomed out, you see the shape of the garden; zoomed in, you read it.

## Without a mouse

`Tab` into the graph and use the arrow keys to move from note to note; `Enter` opens one. A screen reader reads every note with its neighbours as a list.
