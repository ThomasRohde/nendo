# How a note grows

Every note has a stage, shown as a coloured dot in the tree, on the note and in the graph.

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Seed** | A name and little else, often planted by a link to a note that did not exist yet. |
| **Growing** | Being written and linked. Most notes live here. |
| **Evergreen** | Says what it means and is worth linking to. |

## Moving a note along

Open a note's menu in the Garden view and choose **Mark evergreen**. On the note's record page you will also find **Mark growing**, **Tended today**, **Pin** and **Unpin**. Mark evergreen, Mark growing and Tended today record today as the day the note was tended, which is what **Tended lately** on the front page shows.

## Seeds waiting to grow

A link to a note that is not there yet plants a Seed, so nothing in the garden points nowhere. The front page counts the seeds that wait, and the **By stage** board shows them in a column of their own. Pick one, write a few lines, and move it to Growing.

> A good habit: once a week, open **By stage**, take the seed with the most backlinks, and write it. Those are the ideas your other notes keep asking for.

## Lonely notes

A note with no links in or out is an orphan. The **Orphans** screen lists them and the graph can show or hide them. Link an orphan to one other note and the graph pulls it into the garden.

## Kinds of note

A note also has a kind: **Note**, **Map** (a note that gathers others, shown on the **Maps** gallery), **Daily**, **Source** (something you read) and **Template** (the body a new daily note starts with). The graph can colour notes by kind instead of stage.
