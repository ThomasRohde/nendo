# Daily notes and tasks

## One note a day

**Today** in the Garden view opens the note for today. The first time each day it plants a new one, titled with the date and filled from the note whose kind is **Template**. Change that template note and every new day starts the way you like.

Daily notes carry their date, so they sit on the **Daily** calendar. Keep what happened, what you read and what to come back to, and link out with `[[...]]`: a week later, the backlinks on any note show the days you thought about it.

## Tasks live in notes

A line starting with `- [ ]` is a task. Saving makes it a **Task** record that remembers its note, so every task in the garden is in one place:

| Screen | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | The tasks still to do, soonest due first |
| **Due** | Tasks on a calendar, once they have a due date |
| **By source** | Tasks written in notes beside those added by hand |
| **All tasks** | Everything, done or not |

Tick a box while reading and the tick is saved at once, both in the note and on the task. The strip under a note's title holds the next task to do with its box, and **Show all** lists the rest, the ones added by hand too. Give a task a due date on its own page, and it appears on the **Due** calendar.

> A task added by hand on the Tasks screens is marked **Manual** and is yours: no save of a note ever changes it.
