                  Each dot is a note, sized by how many links it has, coloured by its stage or kind. Drag the background to pan, the wheel to zoom, a note to move it. Hover a note to light it and its neighbours. Click to open it; right-click for its neighbourhood. Tags shows tags as small grey dots.

                  A note opens for reading. Edit (Ctrl E) switches to its Markdown. A [[wikilink]] names another note by its slug or title and becomes a Link record when you save; a note it names that is not there yet is planted as a Seed. A #tag becomes a Tag record, and a line that starts with - [ ] becomes a Task: tick it while reading and it is saved. Links, tags and tasks that somebody wrote by hand are kept as they are.
