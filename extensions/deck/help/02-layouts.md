# Layouts

A layout says what a slide shows and where. Choose one in the **Layout** row under the slide; each picture there is drawn with your slide's own title. A layout uses only some of the fields, and the **Slide** tab shows just those.

## The body is a small Markdown

| Write | It shows |
| --- | --- |
| `- A point` | A bullet. Two spaces before the dash make a second level. |
| `1. A step` | A numbered line. |
| `### Heading` | A small heading, such as the top of a column. |
| `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `` `code` `` | Bold, italic and code. |
| `- 42% \| what it counts \| where from` | Parts of one line, split by a bar, for Big numbers, Agenda, Timeline, Process, Cycle and Cards. |

## The twenty-one layouts

| Layout | Use it for | Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | The first slide | One line: who, where, when |
| **Section** | The start of a part | Kicker is the number, such as 02 |
| **Statement** | One idea, said big | No body: the title is the idea |
| **Title and text** | Points or a short paragraph | Bullets |
| **Title only** | A diagram, a screen, or space to draw in PowerPoint | No body: a picture fills the space under the title |
| **Two columns** | Two thoughts side by side | Left in Body, right in Body 2 |
| **Comparison** | Before and after, this and that | Each side starts with `### Heading` |
| **Image left**, **Image right** | Words beside a picture | Bullets |
| **Full image** | A picture that carries the slide | No body |
| **Picture grid** | Two to four pictures side by side | One caption per picture: `- The harbour at dawn` |
| **Quote** | Somebody's words | The quote; the kicker says who |
| **Big numbers** | Two to four figures | `- 42% \| label \| source` |
| **Agenda** | What the talk covers | `- Item \| note` |
| **Timeline** | Steps in time | `- When \| What \| detail` |
| **Process** | Steps that lead on, left to right | `- Step \| detail`, two to six |
| **Cycle** | Steps that come back to the first | `- Step \| detail`, three to six |
| **Cards** | Two to six parallel ideas | `- Heading \| text` |
| **Table** | Rows and columns | A Markdown table |
| **Chart** | Numbers as a column, bar, line, area, stacked, pie or doughnut chart | A Markdown table; Body 2 is the takeaway. A pie or a doughnut draws the first number column |
| **Closing** | Thanks, or the ask | Contact lines |

## When the words do not fit

Deck makes the text smaller until it fits. If it is at its smallest size and still runs over, a line under the stage says so. Shorten the text, split the slide, or move the detail to the speaker notes.

## Pictures

On a slide with a picture, choose **Choose picture…** or drop a picture on the stage. The picture is kept in the file, not linked, so the deck travels with it. Write what it shows under **Picture text**: screen readers read it, and both exports keep it. A picture dropped on a slide without a picture place turns it into **Image right**.

A **Picture grid** has up to four pictures. Drop a picture on a place in the grid, or click the place, to put a picture there. You can also use **Choose picture…** and **More pictures** on the **Slide** tab. Write the captions in **Captions**, one line for each picture, in the same order. A screen reader reads the caption for its picture.
