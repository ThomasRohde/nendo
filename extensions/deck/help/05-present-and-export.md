# Present and export

## Present

**Present** (`F5`) starts at the first slide; **From this slide** (`Shift` `F5`) starts at the one you are on. Double-click a slide in the list to present from it. Hidden slides are left out.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `→`, `Space`, `Page Down`, a click | The next point, then the next slide |
| `←`, `Page Up` | Back a point, then the slide before |
| `Home`, `End` | First and last slide |
| A number, then `Enter` | That slide |
| `P` | The presenter view |
| `L` | A laser pointer that follows the mouse |
| `D` | A pen to draw on the slide; `E` clears what you drew |
| `B`, `W` | A black or a white screen, and back. In the presenter view, a notice over the slide says what the audience sees |
| `T` | Pause the timer, and go on |
| `G` | All slides as a grid; click one to go there |
| `F` | Full screen |
| `Esc` | Put the laser or the pen down, leave the grid or the presenter view, then stop presenting |
| `?` | These keys |

## Points one by one, and transitions

On the **Slide** tab, under **Presenting**, **Points** sets how a slide's points come on: **All at once**, or **One by one**, a click each. One by one brings on a body's bullets, each card, big number, agenda item, timeline, process and cycle step, each picture of a grid, a table's rows and a chart's bars. The title and the rest of the slide are there from the start. While a slide builds, the stage numbers the first thing each click brings on.

**Transition** is how the slide comes on: **Fade**, **Push** or **Zoom**, or **None**. **Deck** takes the deck's own transition, which you set on the **Design** tab under **Presenting**. To set points or a transition on several slides at once, select them in the slide list first.

## The presenter view

`P`, or **Presenter** in the row at the foot of the slides, opens the presenter view. It shows:

- the slide as the audience sees it, and below it every slide, to go to one with a click;
- what comes next: the next point, or the next slide;
- your notes, with **A−** and **A+** to make them smaller or larger;
- the time you have talked, the time on this slide, and the clock;
- the pace: ahead, on time or behind, and the time left, when the deck has a talk length or rehearsal times.

The laser and the pen work on the presenter view's slide too. Inside Nendo the presenter view and the slides share one screen. To show them on two screens, export the HTML file, open it in a browser, press `P` and choose **Audience window**. Drag that window to the projector and press `F` there. The presenter view drives it, and the keys work in either window.

## Export

**Export** on the toolbar offers three files. Each is made from the same drawing as the stage, so what you see is what you get.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **PowerPoint (.pptx)** | Editable slides: text boxes, shapes and charts you can change in PowerPoint, the speaker notes, the template's colours and fonts as the file's theme, and your pictures. Points that come one by one are PowerPoint fade animations on click, and each slide keeps its transition. Hidden slides stay in it, hidden. |
| **HTML (one file)** | The deck as one web page with every picture inside it. It plays in any browser with the keys above: points one by one, transitions, the presenter view, the audience window, the laser and the pen. Print it, or save it as PDF, and you get one slide per page. |
| **Speaker notes (.md)** | The notes of every shown slide, in order, as a text file. |

The files go to your Downloads folder. A picture that is only linked, not kept in the file, is left out of the PowerPoint file, and Deck says so. The HTML file keeps a web picture as a link, which shows when the file is opened online, and leaves out a picture linked from a folder on your computer, and Deck says so too.
