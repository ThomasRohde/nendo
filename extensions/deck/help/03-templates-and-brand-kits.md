# Templates and brand kits

The **Design** tab gives a deck its look. A **template** sets the fonts, the colours and the shapes. A **brand kit** puts your organisation's colours, fonts and logo on top of the template.

## Six templates

| Template | Its look |
| --- | --- |
| **Keynote** | A dark stage, one bright accent, big type. For talks and launches. |
| **Editorial** | Warm paper, serif headlines and rules, like a long read. |
| **Swiss** | A white grid, heavy grotesque type and one red. |
| **Boardroom** | Navy and white with a quiet band. For reviews and board papers. |
| **Studio** | Soft gradients and rounded cards. For product and design stories. |
| **Minimal** | Hairlines, light type and room to breathe. |

Each template is a record. Open one from **Templates** to change its fonts or colours, or copy one to make your own: a copy keeps its family's shapes and takes your colours and fonts.

## A brand kit from Brandkits

A Nendo file cannot read another file, so a kit comes across as an export.

1. In **Brandkits**, open the kit and choose **Export**. Pick the **Claude Design bundle (zip)**, the **design tokens (JSON)** or the **CSS variables**.
2. In Deck, on the **Design** tab, choose **Import from Brandkits…** and pick that file, or drop it on the tab.

Deck reads the kit's primary, secondary and accent colours, its background, surface, text and muted colours, its heading, body and mono fonts, its voice and tagline, and, from the bundle, its logo and its logo for dark backgrounds. The deck you are on starts using the kit at once. Import the same kit again, after a change in Brandkits, and Deck updates what the file holds and keeps the rest.

A kit's logo shows large on the title and closing slides and small at the top of most others; on a dark slide Deck uses the logo for dark backgrounds. A logo picture on its own, dropped on the tab, goes onto the kit the deck uses.

## Colours stay legible

A brand's colours can clash with a template: dark text on a dark stage, or a pale accent on white. Deck checks each colour against the slide behind it and darkens or lightens it until it can be read. A dark template keeps its dark stage and uses the kit's colours as accents.

## Fonts

Deck draws with the fonts your computer has. A font from Google Fonts loads when you are online. PowerPoint uses the fonts of the computer that opens the file, so give the file to people who have the brand's fonts, or expect PowerPoint to substitute them.
