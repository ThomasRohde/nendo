# Arrange slides

## Select

Click a slide to select it. `Ctrl` and a click adds a slide or takes it out; `Shift` and a click selects every slide from the last one you clicked. `Ctrl` `A` selects them all. The slide on the stage is always one of them.

## Move

Drag the selected slides. A line shows where they will land, and the list scrolls when you hold them near its top or bottom. They land in their order, as one block. `Esc` puts them back while you drag.

Hold `Ctrl` when you drop to copy them there instead.

The keys move them too: `Ctrl` `↑` and `Ctrl` `↓` one place, `Ctrl` `Shift` `↑` and `Ctrl` `Shift` `↓` to the start or the end.

## Cut, copy and paste

`Ctrl` `X`, `Ctrl` `C` and `Ctrl` `V` in the slide list cut, copy and paste the selected slides. They paste after the selected slides, into this deck or another deck, also in another Deck file. A picture stays behind when it belongs to another file. Copied slides also paste as text in other programs: the kicker, title and body of each. Cut and copy take up to 100 slides at a time.

## The slide sorter

**Slide sorter** in the toolbar shows every slide at once. Drag, select, cut, copy and paste as in the list. **Size**, or `Ctrl` and the mouse wheel, makes the slides larger or smaller. Double-click a slide or press `Enter` to open it; `Esc` goes back.

## The slides' menu

Right-click a slide, or press `Shift` `F10`, for: new slide, duplicate, cut, copy, paste, hide, move to the start, up, down or to the end, select all, the slide sorter and delete. It works on every selected slide.

## Undo

`Ctrl` `Z` takes back the last change, and `Ctrl` `Y` puts it back: moving, adding, pasting, duplicating, hiding and deleting slides, a layout, and the words you typed on a slide, in the order you made them. If you typed in a slide after you moved it, Undo still puts it back where it was and keeps the words.
