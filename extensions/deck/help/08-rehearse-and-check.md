# Rehearse and check

## Rehearse

**Rehearse** on the toolbar, or on the **Check** tab, presents the deck from the first slide with the presenter view open and its clock running. Talk it through as you will on the day. When you choose **Finish rehearsal**, or press `Esc`, Deck says how long it took and offers to keep the time on each slide. **Keep the times** writes them to the slides as one change, which `Ctrl` `Z` takes back. A slide you did not reach keeps the time it had.

When you present, the presenter view holds the talk to those times. With a **Talk length** on the **Design** tab, it fits the times to that length; without rehearsal times, it shares the length out over the slides.

## Check

The **Check** tab lists what would trip the talk, slide by slide. The number on the tab counts the slides to look at.

| Mark | What it finds |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Text that runs over at its smallest size |
| Amber | A slide with no title, an empty body, a missing picture, or more than 60 words |
| Grey | A slide with no notes, a picture with no picture text, or points one by one on a layout with nothing to bring on |

Click a line to go to its slide. Above the list, Deck says about how long the talk runs: the rehearsal times where there are some, and the notes at 130 words a minute where there are none, against the talk length when there is one.
