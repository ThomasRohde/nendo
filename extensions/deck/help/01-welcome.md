# Welcome to Deck

Deck makes slides. You write the words; the layout places them and the template gives them a look. Your own agent can write a whole deck, or rework one slide, when you ask it.

## Start in four moves

1. **Make a deck.** Choose **New deck**, the plus beside the deck's name in the toolbar. Give it a title, say what the talk is about, and pick a template.
2. **Let the agent write it, or write it yourself.** **Ask the agent to write it** sends your brief to your agent, which writes the slides into the file while you watch. **Start blank** gives you a title slide to build on.
3. **Shape each slide.** Pick a slide on the left and choose its layout in the row under it. Click any words on the slide and type, as in PowerPoint. Right-click them for bold, size, alignment and bullets.
4. **Present or export.** **Present** (`F5`) shows the deck full screen. **Export** makes one HTML file or a PowerPoint file.

## Where things are

| Place | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Slides**, on the left | Every slide in order. Drag slides to move them, select more with `Ctrl` or `Shift`, and right-click for the slides' menu. **Slide sorter** in the toolbar shows them all at once. A purple number on a slide counts the variants your agent wrote for it. |
| **The stage**, in the middle | The slide as it will look. Click words to edit them where they are; right-click for the text menu. Drop a picture on it to put the picture in the slide. |
| **Layout**, under the slide | The twenty-one layouts, each drawn with your slide's title. Click one to give the slide that layout. |
| **Speaker notes**, under the layouts | What you will say, with a count of its words. Presenter view shows them; PowerPoint keeps them. |
| **The ask bar**, at the bottom | Ask your agent for work on the whole deck or on this slide, or pick one of the **Missions**. |
| **Slide**, **Design** and **Agent**, on the right | Whether the slide fits, its fields and the deck's design; the deck's template, brand kit and details; and every ask with its answer. |
| **Decks**, **Brand kits**, **Templates**, **Asks** | The same records as lists, to sort, filter and open. |

The **Example: A tour of Deck** deck uses one layout per slide. Read it, change it, or delete it.
