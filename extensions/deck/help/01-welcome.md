# Welcome to Deck

Deck makes slides. You write the words; the layout places them and the template gives them a look. Your own agent can write a whole deck, or rework one slide, when you ask it.

## Start in four moves

1. **Make a deck.** Choose **New deck**. Give it a title, say what the talk is about, and pick a template.
2. **Let the agent write it, or write it yourself.** **Ask the agent to write it** sends your brief to your agent, which writes the slides into the file while you watch. **Start blank** gives you a title slide to build on.
3. **Shape each slide.** Pick a slide on the left. On the right, choose its layout and type its words. The slide changes as you type.
4. **Present or export.** **Present** (`F5`) shows the deck full screen. **Export** makes one HTML file or a PowerPoint file.

## Where things are

| Place | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Slides**, on the left | Every slide in order. Drag one to move it. A purple number on a slide counts the variants your agent wrote for it. |
| **The stage**, in the middle | The slide as it will look. Click a text to go to its field. Drop a picture on it to put the picture in the slide. |
| **Speaker notes**, under the stage | What you will say. Presenter view shows them; PowerPoint keeps them. |
| **The ask bar**, at the bottom | Ask your agent for work on the whole deck, on this slide, or a mission. |
| **Slide**, **Design** and **Asks**, on the right | The slide's layout and fields; the deck's template, brand kit and details; and every ask with its answer. |
| **Decks**, **Brand kits**, **Templates**, **Asks** | The same records as lists, to sort, filter and open. |

The **Example: A tour of Deck** deck uses one layout per slide. Read it, change it, or delete it.
